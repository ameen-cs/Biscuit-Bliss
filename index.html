The Selection

Every tub
tells on itself.

Drag across. Each one is baked, dipped, drizzled and packed by hand — no two trays come out identical, and that is rather the point.

Coffee Pecan

Freshly baked coffee pecan biscuits, half dipped in silky chocolate. Simple, delicious, and just the right amount of indulgence.

Pistachio Selection

Delicately nutty, perfectly buttery. Sold as four varieties packed together in one tub.

Mocha Biscuits

The ultimate indulgence for our coffee lovers — piped chocolate shortbread, drizzled and finished with chopped nuts.

Choc Chip Cookies

Soft-centred, properly loaded, still warm when they come off the rack. The one everybody asks for first.

Nieman Marcus

Baked to perfection and finished with a fine white chocolate drizzle. Oaty, chewy, quietly addictive.

Milk Chocolate Pecan

Melt-in-your-mouth all-butter biscuits paired with pecans and dipped in milk chocolate. Only available in the Limited Edition tub.

Chocolate Biscotti

Twice-baked, packed with nuts and dark chocolate, cut thick. Built for dunking.

Nougat Fusion

Homemade nougat with the perfect blend of premium almonds, pecans and cashew nuts. The one that gets given as a gift.

Pastry Muska

A firm favourite. Flaky, sesame-topped and impossible to resist — simple, delicious, gone by the afternoon.

Our story

A biscuit
connoisseur.

Biscuit Bliss started the way most good things in Durban kitchens do — one tray at a time, for people who could tell the difference.

Nothing here comes out of a machine. The dough is mixed in small batches, the chocolate is tempered and hand-dipped, the pistachio is crushed fresh, and every tub is labelled by hand before it leaves. It is slower. It is also the entire reason the tubs empty as fast as they do.

Ten thousand people follow the trays coming out of that oven. We would rather you tasted one.
